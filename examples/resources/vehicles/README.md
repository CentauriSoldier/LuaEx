# Vehicle illustrations

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the LuaEx vehicle example. Each
asset was generated separately and copied here at its original resolution.
The illustrations depict example presets; they do not imply extra framework
component slots or vehicle subclasses.

| File | Preset | Art direction / generation prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rover.png` | Trailwarden | Wide premium painterly game illustration of an amber six-wheel expedition rover, cream cabin, roof rack and exposed mechanics, on a rocky mountain trail. Warm afternoon light, teal shadows. No people, logos, text or UI. |
| `airship.png` | Cloudrunner | Wide painterly game illustration of a teal, ivory and brass propeller cargo airship with a wooden cargo cabin and twin props, hovering above a mooring platform in a mountain valley. Golden light. No people, text, logos or UI. |
| `skiff.png` | Stillwater | Wide painterly game illustration of an indigo and copper autonomous survey motor skiff, cream enclosed cabin, instrument mast and visible engine, on a turquoise lake among forested cliffs. Warm golden light. No people, text, logos or UI. |

The workbench embeds the PNGs in its offline bundle and renders them with
descriptive alternative text. Image generation originals remain in the tool's
generated-images directory.
